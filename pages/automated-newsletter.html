Setting up an Automated Newsletter

Connect signup forms, audience lists, content sources, and scheduled email delivery.

YOUR FIELD GUIDE

What to explore

Connect signup forms, audience lists, content sources, and scheduled email delivery. Use this guide to compare options, understand the main decisions, and plan a sensible first version of your project.

Tools and examples to research

  • Create signup form
  • Store consent
  • Prepare email template
  • Test automation

Questions to ask before choosing

  • Does the tool fit the project you actually want to build?
  • Can you export or back up your information?
  • Are the important features available on the plan you can afford?
  • Can you test the complete user experience before launch?
Builder tip: Use confirmed opt-in where appropriate, include unsubscribe options, and test the full subscriber journey.

Suggested workflow
